The harness

Sovereign. Your money never answers to us.

An autonomous trading harness. You bring the broker account, the AI model, and the server. Sovereign brings the rails, the watchdogs, and a memory that learns from its own trades. Optimized for the Claude family of models.

A handspike was the lever bar of the age of sail — worked at the capstan to haul the anchor, and at the gun carriage to lay the cannon on target. One person moving something far too heavy by hand, with precision. That is the whole company in one tool: leverage and aim.

Why a harness

Handed real money, every frontier model in a public study lost it.

In a 57-day public study, six frontier models were each given $10,000 of real capital on a live market venue. All six lost money — down between 16.0% and 30.8% — and the amount of research they did had no correlation with their results. Our reading of that result: the mind alone is not the product. The mind alone is an accident waiting to happen.

Sovereign is the machinery built to stand between an intelligent model and an expensive mistake: limits enforced outside the model's control, watchdogs that assume the model is down until proven otherwise, and accounting built not to flatter itself.

  • Missile covers — hard software gates modeled on the covers over a launch switch; nothing trades live until a human deliberately arms it, and every cover is designed to fail closed.
  • Deterministic risk rails — per-class position caps, rules that never permit an uncovered position or a position without a stop, and a drawdown backstop that halts trading at a preset loss threshold.
  • Watchdogs that don't use AI — a no-LLM enforcer on a timer and an independent last-resort sentinel, both built on the assumption that the mind can go dark mid-position.
  • Truth accounting — P&L measured so your own deposits can never be mistaken for gains, append-only ledgers, trades recorded at real prices with real fees. A book that flatters itself is worse than no book.
  • A memory system — every scar, every lesson, every ledger entry accrues on your machine, from your own trades. Your Sovereign becomes yours.
